claude-watch 적용 전 원래 statusLine 명령 백업 (복원용)
복원하려면 ~/.claude/settings.json 의 "statusLine".command 값을 아래로 되돌리면 됩니다.

원래 statusLine (claude-hud 단독):

bash -c 'cols=$(stty size </dev/tty 2>/dev/null | awk '"'"'{print $2}'"'"'); export COLUMNS=$(( ${cols:-120} > 4 ? ${cols:-120} - 4 : 1 )); plugin_dir=$(ls -d "${CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR:-$HOME/.claude}"/plugins/cache/*/claude-hud/*/ 2>/dev/null | awk -F/ '"'"'{ print $(NF-1) "\t" $(0) }'"'"' | grep -E '"'"'^[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+[[:space:]]'"'"' | sort -t. -k1,1n -k2,2n -k3,3n -k4,4n | tail -1 | cut -f2-); exec "/opt/homebrew/bin/node" "${plugin_dir}dist/index.js"'

JSON에 넣을 때(이스케이프된 형태):
"bash -c 'cols=$(stty size </dev/tty 2>/dev/null | awk '\"'\"'{print $2}'\"'\"'); export COLUMNS=$(( ${cols:-120} > 4 ? ${cols:-120} - 4 : 1 )); plugin_dir=$(ls -d \"${CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR:-$HOME/.claude}\"/plugins/cache/*/claude-hud/*/ 2>/dev/null | awk -F/ '\"'\"'{ print $(NF-1) \"\\t\" $(0) }'\"'\"' | grep -E '\"'\"'^[0-9]+\\.[0-9]+\\.[0-9]+[[:space:]]'\"'\"' | sort -t. -k1,1n -k2,2n -k3,3n -k4,4n | tail -1 | cut -f2-); exec \"/opt/homebrew/bin/node\" \"${plugin_dir}dist/index.js\"'"
